About

Working with private clients, interior designers and galleries seeking exceptional, accessible pieces for considered spaces. Trained at the Mosaic School of Spilimbergo, Anna Rommel went on to co-restore the ceiling mosaics of Notre Dame de la Garde in Marseille. She is part of Atelier Céladon, an artist collective based in Marseille.
Every piece is unique and handmade.
