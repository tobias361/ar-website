Design & Mosaïque

Based between Forcalquier and Marseille,
her work moves between bespoke furniture and jewellery — confronting ancient Mediterranean craft with contemporary concepts and matter.

About

Working with private clients, interior designers and galleries seeking exceptional, accessible pieces for considered spaces. Every piece is unique and handmade.

Trained at the Mosaic School of Spilimbergo, Anna Rommel went on to co-restore the ceiling mosaics of Notre Dame de la Garde. She is part of Atelier Céladon, an artist collective based in Marseille.
