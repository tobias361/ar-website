About

Trained at the Mosaic School of Spilimbergo, Anna Rommel went on to co-restore the ceiling mosaics of Notre Dame de la Garde. She is part of Atelier Céladon, an artist collective based in Marseille. Working with private clients, interior designers and galleries seeking exceptional, accessible pieces for considered spaces.
